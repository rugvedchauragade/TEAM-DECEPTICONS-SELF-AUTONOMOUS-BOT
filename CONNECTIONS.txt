=====================================================================
 SELF-BALANCING PICK & DROP ROBOT  -  CONNECTIONS
 (matches self_balancing_robot.ino exactly)
=====================================================================

IMPORTANT RULES
  1. ALL grounds (GND) must be joined together: battery, buck, ESP32,
     TB6612, MPU6050, both servos.
  2. Set the buck converter to 5.0 V with a multimeter BEFORE
     connecting the ESP32 or servos.
  3. Do NOT power servos from an ESP32 pin. Use the buck 5 V rail.
  4. Do NOT plug in USB while the robot's battery is also feeding
     the ESP32 VIN pin. Switch the robot OFF when uploading code by USB.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. POWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   Li-ion cell 1 (+) --- cell 2 (-)      <- two cells in SERIES (7.4 V,
   Cell 2 (+) ----------------------        8.4 V when fully charged)
   Cell 1 (-) ----------------------

   Battery pack (+) --> [ON/OFF SWITCH] --> "SWITCHED +"
   Battery pack (-) -------------------------------------> "GND"

   Charger / 2S BMS module connects to the battery pack
   (follow the module's own markings: B+, B-, P+, P-, etc.)

   SWITCHED +  -->  Buck converter IN+     (GND -> Buck IN-)
   SWITCHED +  -->  TB6612FNG  VM          (motor power, 7.4 V direct)

   Buck converter OUT+ (5.0 V) -->  ESP32 VIN (5V) pin
                               -->  Servo 1 red wire
                               -->  Servo 2 red wire
                               -->  470-1000 uF capacitor (+)
   Buck converter OUT-  (GND)  -->  ESP32 GND, servo brown wires,
                                    capacitor (-), and the main GND

   ESP32 3V3 pin  -->  MPU6050 VCC
                  -->  TB6612FNG VCC (logic)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. ESP32  <-->  TB6612FNG  (motor driver)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   ESP32 GPIO     TB6612FNG pin     Function
   ----------     -------------     ---------------------------
   GPIO 13        STBY              Standby / enable
   GPIO 26        AIN1              Left motor direction 1
   GPIO 25        AIN2              Left motor direction 2
   GPIO 27        PWMA              Left motor speed (PWM)
   GPIO 33        BIN1              Right motor direction 1
   GPIO 32        BIN2              Right motor direction 2
   GPIO 4         PWMB              Right motor speed (PWM)
   3V3            VCC               Logic power
   GND            GND               Ground (all GND pins)
   Battery +      VM                Motor power (7.4 V)

   TB6612FNG pin     Goes to
   -------------     ----------------------------
   A01, A02          LEFT  N20 motor (2 wires)
   B01, B02          RIGHT N20 motor (2 wires)

   Tip: solder a 100 uF capacitor across VM and GND near the TB6612.
   If a wheel spins the wrong way, DO NOT rewire: change
   MOTOR_L_INVERT / MOTOR_R_INVERT in the code.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. ESP32  <-->  MPU6050 (GY-521 module)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   ESP32 pin      MPU6050 pin
   ---------      -----------
   3V3            VCC
   GND            GND
   GPIO 21        SDA
   GPIO 22        SCL
   (not used)     INT, XDA, XCL
   GND (or free)  AD0   -> I2C address 0x68 (what the code uses)

   MOUNTING: fix the board FLAT (horizontal), close to the wheel
   axle height, with the X arrow printed on the board pointing to the
   FRONT of the robot. Keep it rigid - no wobble, no foam tape.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. ESP32  <-->  SERVOS (2x MG90S)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   ESP32 GPIO     Servo
   ----------     ------------------------------------
   GPIO 18        ARM / LIFT servo   - signal (orange/yellow)
   GPIO 19        GRIPPER servo      - signal (orange/yellow)

   Both servos:   red   -> Buck 5 V
                  brown -> GND (common)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. QUICK PIN SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------------------------

   GPIO 4   PWMB          GPIO 22  MPU SCL
   GPIO 13  STBY          GPIO 25  AIN2
   GPIO 18  Arm servo     GPIO 26  AIN1
   GPIO 19  Gripper servo GPIO 27  PWMA
   GPIO 21  MPU SDA       GPIO 32  BIN2
                          GPIO 33  BIN1


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. BLOCK DIAGRAM
---------------------------------------------------------------------

                          +------------------+
   2x Li-ion (7.4V) ----> | SWITCH | CHARGER |
                          +--------+---------+
                                   |
                  +----------------+----------------+
                  |                                 |
            +-----v------+                    +-----v------+
            | BUCK 5.0 V |                    |  TB6612FNG |--> Left N20
            +--+----+----+                    |  (VM=7.4V) |--> Right N20
               |    |                         +-----^------+
        +------v-+  +--> Servo 1 + Servo 2          | 6 control wires
        | ESP32  |------------------------------------+ + STBY
        | VIN    |--- I2C (21,22) ---> MPU6050 (3V3)
        +--------+--- GPIO 18, 19 ---> servo signals
