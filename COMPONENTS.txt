=====================================================================
 SELF-BALANCING PICK & DROP ROBOT  -  COMPONENTS LIST
=====================================================================

 #  QTY  COMPONENT                      NOTES
--- ---  -------------------------      -------------------------------
 1   1   ESP32 DevKit (30 or 38 pin)    Wi-Fi hotspot + brain. "ESP32 Dev
                                        Module" board in Arduino IDE.
 2   1   MPU6050 module (GY-521)        Gyro + accelerometer (I2C).
 3   1   TB6612FNG motor driver module  Dual H-bridge, 1.2 A per channel.
 4   2   N20 gear motors                6 V, about 200-300 RPM is a good
                                        choice. (12 V motors are weak on a
                                        7.4 V battery.)
 5   2   N20 wheels (about 40-43 mm)    Rubber tyres for grip. Bigger
                                        wheels are easier to balance.
 6   2   N20 motor brackets / mounts    To fix motors to the chassis.
 7   2   MG90S servo motors             1 = arm lift, 1 = gripper. Metal
                                        gear version recommended.
 8   1   DC-DC buck converter (5 V)     At least 3 A (LM2596 or similar).
                                        Powers ESP32 + both servos.
 9   2   Li-ion rechargeable cells      18650, 3.7 V each, same brand and
                                        same charge level. Wired in SERIES
                                        = 7.4 V (8.4 V full).
10   1   2-cell battery holder          Series-wired 2x18650 holder.
11   1   Battery charger module         Must support 2S (2 cells in series,
                                        8.4 V). A 2S BMS board with charge
                                        input is best. A TP4056 is 1-cell
                                        only: do NOT use it on a 2S pack.
12   1   ON/OFF switch                  Rocker or slide switch, 3 A or more.
                                        Placed on the battery + wire.
13   1   Capacitor 470-1000 uF, 16 V    On the 5 V rail near the servos.
14   1   Capacitor 100 uF, 16 V         Across TB6612 VM and GND.
15   1   Gripper mechanism              3D-printed or acrylic claw driven by
                                        one MG90S. Keep it LIGHT.
16   1   Arm / lift mechanism           Driven by the other MG90S.
17   1   Robot chassis (2-3 layers)     Battery on the upper/middle layer,
                                        electronics in the middle, arm on
                                        top. Rigid, no flex.
18   1   Perfboard or mini breadboard   Prefer soldered or screw-terminal
                                        connections: loose jumper wires
                                        cause falls.
19   1   Jumper wires / hook-up wire    Female-female, plus 22 AWG for
                                        motor and power lines.
20   1   Screws, nuts, standoffs        M2 and M3.
21   1   USB cable (data type)          For uploading code to the ESP32.


 OPTIONAL BUT HELPFUL
 --------------------
   - Heat-shrink tube and a soldering iron.
   - Terminal blocks (screw type) for power distribution.
   - Fuse (3 A) on the battery + line.
   - Double-sided tape or Velcro for the battery pack.
   - Multimeter (to set the buck converter to exactly 5.0 V).
   - Small weight (washers or coins) to help find the best centre of
     gravity.


 TOOLS
 -----
   Computer with Arduino IDE (ESP32 board package installed),
   screwdriver set, soldering iron, wire stripper, multimeter.

 BUILD TIPS
 ----------
   - Keep the centre of gravity HIGH and the body narrow: the robot
     balances more easily with weight higher above the axle, so put the
     battery up, not down on the floor.
   - Fix the MPU6050 firmly and flat, with its X arrow pointing forward.
   - Use two identical wheels and tyres.
   - Fully charge the battery before tuning; low voltage changes how
     the motors respond.
